Who am I?

My name is Taehyun Wilmoth, and I am an aspiring Chef/Restaurateur. I have about 7 years of experience working in the restaurant industry, and I hope to never stop.

Working with food brings me a sense of peace that I don't feel doing anything else. Even in a chaotic kitchen when people are shouting at each other. I feel nothing but at home. It is my calling in life.

I truly hope that you all have enjoyed reading through this website thus far. Maybe I will start a food blog in the near future to document the rest of my culinary journey.

My Personal History

I have have had a love for making food since I was a young child. I grew up watching all of the cooking and baking shows I could get my eyes on, admiring the work of the famous professionals and the competitors alike.

While I'd bake and decorate cakes with my mom growing up, I didn't seriously start cooking for myself until I was about 12 years old. One of the first things I ever made was spaghetti. A simple dish with jarred sauce, but the process of learning kept me inspired.

I got my first job at 16 working in a restaurant up at Rock City called Cafe 7. This is where I really started to fall in love with, not just cooking at home, but working in the industry. Over the next year and a half, I worked my way up from Dish Pit, to Salad Station then to the Line itself. The Chef that I worked under, Chef Carey Douglas, saw the potential I had and taught me everything she could. When I announced my decision to pursue Culinary School, she was the one to write my recommendation letter.

In 2021, I attempted to take on The Culinary Institute of America to get a Bachelor's in Restaruant Business Management. I entered with the largest class that school had ever seen. I had the college credits to start as a Sophomore and finish my degree in 3 years instead of 4. The photos you saw above are from my time there.

To keep a long story short, I had to drop out after my first semester due to health issues. On top of a recovering knee-injury (where I had to pretty much re-learn how to use my right leg) I discovered that I had solid case of Degenerative Disc Disease in my spine. AKA, I had an old-man back at only 19 years of age. This crushed me mentally for a very long time. I wasn't sure if I could stay in the industry that my heart called to.

Despite this, I have kept pressing on my culinary journey. Both cooking for myself at home, experimenting and developing my skills, and trying to work in various places as I re-built my physical strength.

As of now, I am still a ways away from my goal of owning my own restaurant (or multiple). But every day I get a little bit closer to it.

In the last 6 months, I got sober, I found a new job, and I started back in school. Right now, I am aiming to get an associates in Business Management here at Chatt State. For the first time in a long time, I feel confident in my abilities.

I am SO thankful that I have a mind full of so many ideas and concepts to choose from. I can't wait to see what I do with the rest of my life, and I hope that anyone who has read this far gets a chance to eat something that I have made.

Whatever you do, just keep pushing.

Thank you for reading.

-Tae
