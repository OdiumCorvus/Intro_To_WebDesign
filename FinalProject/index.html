A Brief History

Chattanooga, TN is an ever-evolving city with a rich culinary history. From classic Southern Comfort Food, the emergence of "The Working Man's Lunch" aka: a Moon-Pie paired with an RC Cola, to the invention of the signature square patty of Krystal's, Chattanooga has solidified itself as a hub of Southern American Culture. One of the oldest restaurants in the city was Zarzour's Cafe. family owned for the entirety of their existence (1918-2025), it was a hole-in-the-wall spot that specialized in staple Southern Comfort Food and was most known for their great hospitality and their burgers. While it is no longer open, there are plenty of other spots such as Old Man Rivers Table & Tavern and Elsie's Daughter to get a taste of the south on your plate.

Modern Chatt

Today, Chattanooga has truly exploded in the culinary scene. People from many different backgrounds and cultures have all come together here to share their foods with each other, and it has become a truly beautiful thing to witness. Even if I tried for hours, I couldn't list all the different types of food you can find here in this city. There are always new concepts, new restaurants, and new food trucks, appearing that it's impossible to keep up.
